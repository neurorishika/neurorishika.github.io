What intensity of motion feels right?

All three options preserve the field notebook aesthetic — they differ in how much the page moves as you scroll.

A
subtle depth

Barely-there parallax

The page still feels like a document — just alive at the edges. The brain SVG drifts upward gently as you scroll past the hero. Marginalia notes have micro-offsets at different rates. Nothing calls attention to itself.

  • Hero brain parallax at 0.2× scroll rate
  • Marginalia float at different offsets per section
  • Scroll-reactive section title opacity/translate (very slight)
B
notebook layers

Physical depth + mouse interaction

The page has multiple layers moving at different rates — like pinned notes at different depths. Cards respond to mouse position with a gentle 3D tilt. Marginalia notes drift at their own speeds. Still academic, but unmistakably interactive.

  • Hero brain parallax at 0.3× + counter-drift on text
  • Tool cards 3D-tilt on mouse hover (subtle perspective)
  • Marginalia drift at different rates (like pinned layers)
  • Scroll-progress-driven underbar animations
C
scroll choreography

Full scroll-driven narrative

Each section has its own entrance choreography tied to scroll progress — not just fade-in but transform sequences. The hero has a proper parallax scene. Section eyebrows draw in from the left as they enter view. More editorial, less notebook.

  • Hero parallax with text + brain at different rates
  • Section eyebrows draw in horizontally on scroll enter
  • Scroll-progress drives color/opacity shifts per section
  • Publication list items stagger on scroll progress
  • Tool cards 3D tilt + card-level scroll animation

My recommendation: B (notebook layers) — it adds clear interactivity without fighting the academic restraint of the field notebook theme.
