effect 01 · scroll-driven
🧠

Hero BrainMap parallax

Brain drifts upward at 0.3× scroll rate. Hero pitch text counter-drifts at −0.08× — creates the illusion of depth layers in the hero.

  • driver useScrollY() → inline transform
  • brain translateY(scrollY × 0.3)
  • text translateY(scrollY × −0.08)
  • clamp stops at hero bottom

effect 02 · mouse-driven
⟲ 3D tilt on hover

Tool card perspective tilt

Cards rotate on X+Y axes as the mouse moves across them — max ±7°. Smooth lerp to flat on mouse leave. transform-origin stays at card center.

  • driver onMouseMove → rotateX/Y
  • max tilt 7° on each axis
  • perspective 900px on .tools-grid
  • reset CSS transition 400ms ease-out
src/Sections.jsx + src/styles.css
effect 03 · spring physics
measurement is half the science

Marginalia damped oscillator

Each note hangs from a pin at its top center. Scroll velocity injects a push; a spring + damper returns it to vertical. Each note has slightly different stiffness so they settle at different rates.

  • driver useSpring() hook (new, in hooks.jsx)
  • axis rotateZ, origin top center
  • stiffness 80–100 (varies per note)
  • damping 7–10 (varies per note)
  • input Δscroll → velocity injection
src/hooks.jsx + src/Sections.jsx
effect 04 · scroll-progress
Academic path

Section title underbar draw-in

The burnt-sienna rule under each section title grows from 0 → 44px as the section header enters view, driven by scroll progress. Currently it just appears; this makes it feel drawn.

  • driver useInView() progress prop
  • from width 0px → 44px
  • easing cubic-bezier(.25,0,.1,1)
  • trigger section-head enters viewport
